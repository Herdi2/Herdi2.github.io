Hello! I'm Herdi. This is my little site, where I show off my projects and (hopefully) write a bit. I am a former student at KTH where I recently obtained a Master's degree in Computer Science. My thesis was in compiler verification using translation validation, applied to the C2 JVM JIT compiler. Before that I'd interned at Ericsson, working on a fuzzer for their in-house compiler.

Although my interests span most of what CS has to offer, I've fallen into functional programming! In fact, this site is fully generated using Haskell. I also wrote my thesis tool, C2tv , in Haskell. Even Ericsson had me working on a fuzzer written in Haskell! It seems the monads are after me...
